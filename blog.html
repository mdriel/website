Do You Keep Having the Same Fight with your Partner?

If you feel like you're having the same argument in different disguises, you're probably noticing something important: patterns. Patterns are the repeated ways we respond when we feel hurt, scared, misunderstood, alone… They show up in tone, timing, and assumptions — and once a pattern takes hold, it can start running the relationship on autopilot.
